-------------------------

EVALUATION CRITERIA

-------------------------

1. Unused or Orphaned Elements:
   - Does the Candidate declare `entities` or `relationships` that are never used in the `target`, `constraint`, `projection`, `order_by`, or as a necessary topological bridge? 
   - Penalize "dead weight" declarations. Every defined node and edge must serve a logical purpose.

2. Redundant Relationships:
   - Are there duplicated relationships between the same entities performing the exact same semantic function?
   - Penalize models that redundantly define bi-directional relationships (e.g., A -> B and B -> A) when a single directional edge inherently covers the graph traversal logic, unless explicitly required by the prompt.

3. Duplicated & Tautological Conditions:
   - Are conditions repeated? (e.g., `year > 2000 AND year > 2000`).
   - Are there tautologies? (e.g., filtering for `attribute_name: "type", operator: "=", right: "Movie"` in the constraints when the entity was already declared with `"type": "Movie"` in the entities array).
   
4. Logical Bloat:
   - Are simple conditions unnecessarily wrapped in deep, redundant logical blocks? (e.g., wrapping a single comparison inside an `and_conditions` which is inside another `and_conditions`).

-------------------------

SCORING METRICS

-------------------------

Assign a continuous score between 0.0 and 1.0:

- 1.0 (Perfect):
  Absolutely minimal and lean. No unused entities, no redundant relationships, and constraints are perfectly optimized without duplication.

- 0.7 - 0.9 (High):
  Mostly minimal. Might contain a very minor inefficiency, such as wrapping a single constraint in an unnecessary `and_conditions` array, but contains no orphaned entities or duplicated logic.

- 0.4 - 0.6 (Partial):
  Noticeable bloat. Declares entities or relationships that are never utilized, or includes tautological filters (e.g., re-verifying the entity type in the constraints). 

- 0.0 - 0.3 (Poor):
  Highly bloated and redundant. Massive duplication of logic, numerous orphaned entities, redundant graph paths, and excessively deep/unnecessary constraint nesting.
