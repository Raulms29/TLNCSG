-------------------------

EVALUATION CRITERIA

-------------------------

1. Entity Coverage:
   - Do all entities mentioned or implied in the NL appear in the IR?
   - Penalize omissions: under-specification of subjects/objects.
   - Penalize hallucinations: invention of entities not present in the NL.

2. Relationship Coverage:
   - Are both explicit and implicit relationships described in the NL modeled?
   - Check whether the connection path (`from` / `to`) and the role accurately reflect the NL action.
   - **Bidirectional Tolerance:** For inherently undirected relationships (e.g., "married to", "neighbor of"), accept a single directional edge (A -> B) as fully faithful. However, for mutually directed actions (e.g., "authors who cited each other"), both directional edges (A -> B AND B -> A) MUST be present to satisfy the semantic constraint.

3. Constraint Capture:
   - Are all mentioned constraints (filters, thresholds, dates, names) present?
   - Verify operator accuracy (e.g., "more than" vs "at least") and quantifier integrity (`ALL` vs `EXISTS`).

4. Intent Preservation:
   - Does the IR answer the same question?
   - Check whether `target` and `projection` match the user's specific information need.

-------------------------

SCORING METRICS

-------------------------

Assign a continuous score between 0.0 and 1.0:

- 1.0 (Perfect):
  Perfect semantic alignment. Captures all nuances, quantifiers, and logical filters.

- 0.7 - 0.9 (High):
  High fidelity. Correct intent and paths, with minor semantic omissions that do not greatly affect the final result.

- 0.4 - 0.6 (Partial):
  Core subjects are present, but critical filters, aggregations, or quantifiers are missing or incorrect.

- 0.0 - 0.3 (Poor):
  Major hallucinations, wrong target, or failure to capture the primary request.
